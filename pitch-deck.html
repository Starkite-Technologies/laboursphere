LabourSphere

Labour expertise, delivered as a system. A technology-enabled labour advisory and workplace solutions business for Namibia.

The problem

Good labour guidance is hard to reach.

Labour matters are complex, and practical specialist help is not always easy or affordable to get.

SMEsOften have no dedicated employee-relations or specialist HR capability.
EmployeesStruggle to understand their rights, responsibilities and options.
The resultProcedural mistakes, weak documentation, compliance gaps and disputes that could have been prevented.
The vision

Not a consultancy that sells time.

A business where professional expertise is turned into intellectual property, systems and repeatable solutions, delivered digitally and at scale.

AccessibleClear answers on any phone or computer.
PracticalTools that solve a defined workplace problem.
AffordablePriced for SMEs and individuals.
Human where it countsProfessionals step in when judgement matters.
Who we serve

Starting in Namibia.

Primary: small and medium businessesNeed reliable labour support but cannot justify a full-time specialist.
EmployeesLooking for accessible workplace information and guidance.
Over timeLarger organisations, HR practitioners, NGOs, professional bodies and training institutions.
Commercial model

Five layers of value.

1. Free educational contentBuilds trust, visibility and authority.
2. Digital productsTemplates, toolkits and checklists sold repeatedly.
3. AI-assisted solutionsGuided help and document support, with safeguards.
4. Professional consultationsFor matters needing assessment and judgement.
5. Bespoke advisoryPolicies, investigations, audits, dispute support, training.
The operating system

One connected system, not a website with a chatbot.

A customer discovers LabourSphere, identifies the problem, gets guidance, pays, completes intake and receives the output, with minimal manual administration.

AI with safeguards

AI amplifies expertise. It does not replace it.

AutomateLead capture, payments, bookings, reminders, product delivery, follow-up, reporting.
AI-assistedIssue identification, general guidance and document support, drawn only from a curated and version-controlled knowledge base.
Keep humanComplex, sensitive or high-risk matters are always escalated to a professional.

Roadmap

Built gradually, over about three years.

Phase 1Build
  • Brand, website, domain
  • Knowledge architecture and IP
  • First digital products
  • CRM and initial automation
Phase 2Validate and automate
  • Test demand and pricing
  • Grow an audience
  • Introduce AI-assisted services
  • Repeatable customer journeys
Phase 3Transition and scale
  • Functioning platform
  • Validated products and revenue
  • Documented processes
  • Founder moves full-time
Minimum viable LabourSphere

The right foundation, without over-investing.

Build now
  • Website with free guides
  • Issue Checker (rule-based)
  • Three to five digital products
  • Simple CRM and email list
Add once demand is proven
  • Online payments and booking
  • Document generator
  • AI assistant on the knowledge base
  • Owner analytics dashboard
Defer
  • Consultations and bespoke advisory, until the founder's employment obligations allow
  • Regional expansion
The outcome
Not "How do I start a consultancy?" but "How do I scale the system I have already built?"

Expertise creates the IP. Technology distributes it. AI amplifies it. Automation runs it. People handle what matters most.
